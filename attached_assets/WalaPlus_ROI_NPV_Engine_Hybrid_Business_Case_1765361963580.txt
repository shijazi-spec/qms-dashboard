WalaPlus ROI & NPV Engine (Hybrid Model)
Business Case & Field Guide
Version 1.0 – For Replit Development

------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------
This document provides the full business case, field structure, explanations, 
backend rules, and UI/UX logic for building the WalaPlus ROI & NPV Engine 
using the Hybrid Model (Simple UI + Expandable Detailed Fields). 
It is designed specifically for SaaS organizations and aligned with 
WalaPlus financial, operational, and governance requirements.

The goal:
- Enable accurate ROI, NPV, and Payback analysis.
- Allow business units to provide inputs easily.
- Give Finance the breakdown needed for validation.
- Allow Agentic AI to validate, correct, and challenge assumptions.
- Provide a unified investment evaluation engine for all departments.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. UI STRUCTURE (HYBRID DESIGN)
------------------------------------------------------------
Fields are grouped into collapsible sections:

A. Basic Required Fields (Always Visible)
B. Manpower Cost Breakdown (Expandable)
C. Platform & Tool Costs (Expandable)
D. Error, Rework & SLA Costs (Expandable)
E. Revenue Impact (Expandable)
F. Implementation Breakdown (Expandable)
G. Risk & Confidence Inputs (Expandable)
H. AI Validation Layer (Auto)

------------------------------------------------------------
A. BASIC REQUIRED FIELDS (EXPLANATIONS)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Initiative Name  
- Simple name describing the initiative.
- Provided by BU Owner.

2. Department  
- Which BU owns the initiative.
- Provided by BU Owner.

3. Problem Statement  
- Short explanation of the issue.
- Provided by Quality + BU Owner.

4. Expected Monthly Savings (High-Level)
- Fast estimation before detailed breakdowns.
- Provided jointly by Finance + BU Owner.

5. High-Level Implementation Cost
- Ballpark before detailed breakdown.
- Provided by IT + Vendor + Finance.

6. Project Duration (Months)
- Estimated delivery timeline.
- Provided by IT/PMO.

7. Priority (High/Medium/Low)
- Assigned by Executive team.

------------------------------------------------------------
B. MANPOWER COST BREAKDOWN (EXPANDABLE)
------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Calculate fully loaded labor cost and time savings.

Fields & Explanations:

1. Average Monthly Salary (SAR)
- From HR.

2. GOSI % (Employer Contribution)
- From Finance (usually 12%).

3. Insurance Cost (SAR/month)
- From HR/Finance.

4. Laptop & Equipment Cost (Amortized/month)
- Laptop cost ÷ 36 months.
- From IT Assets.

5. Software Seat Cost (SAR/month)
- Zoho, Five9, Fluent, etc.
- From Finance + IT.

6. Hours Wasted per Employee per Week
- From BU + Quality observations.

7. Number of Employees Impacted
- From BU.

AUTO-CALCULATIONS:
- Fully Loaded Salary
- Cost per Hour
- Monthly Time Loss Cost = Employees × Hours × Cost per Hour × 4

------------------------------------------------------------
C. PLATFORM & TOOL COSTS (EXPANDABLE)
------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Identify subscription, cloud, or tool savings.

Fields:

1. Current Tools Used  
2. Cost per Tool (SAR/month)  
3. Seats Impacted  
4. Tools to Remove (Duplication)  
5. Vendor Support Cost  

Sources:
- IT + Finance + Vendor Contracts

System auto-calculates total platform cost and potential savings.

------------------------------------------------------------
D. ERROR, REWORK & SLA COSTS (EXPANDABLE)
------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Quantify financial losses from errors & rework.

Fields:

1. Current Error Rate (%)  
2. Monthly Transactions  
3. Cost per Error (SAR)  
4. Rework Hours per Error  
5. SLA Penalties (SAR/month)  
6. Expected Reduction (%)  

Sources:
- Quality Audits
- Operations Metrics
- Finance for penalty amounts

AUTO-CALCULATIONS:
Error Savings = Error Rate Reduction × Transactions × Cost per Error

------------------------------------------------------------
E. REVENUE IMPACT (OPTIONAL - EXPANDABLE)
------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Estimate direct/indirect revenue impact.

Fields:

1. Leads Saved  
2. Conversion Rate (%)  
3. Revenue per Lead (SAR)  
4. Churn Reduction (%)  
5. Upsell Opportunities  

Sources:
- BI Team
- Finance
- Sales
- Customer Success

AUTO-CALCULATIONS:
Revenue Impact = Leads Saved × Conversion Rate × Revenue/Lead

------------------------------------------------------------
F. IMPLEMENTATION BREAKDOWN (EXPANDABLE)
------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Estimate project cost accurately.

Fields:

1. Vendor Costs  
2. Setup Fees  
3. API/License Fees  
4. Internal Development Hours  
   - Developer
   - QA
   - Product
   - Architect
5. Training Cost  
6. Change Management Cost  
7. Monthly Recurring Cost  

Sources:
- IT
- PMO
- Vendors
- Finance

AUTO-CALCULATIONS:
Dev Cost = Hours × (Hourly rate per role)

------------------------------------------------------------
G. RISK & CONFIDENCE INPUTS (EXPANDABLE)
------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Adjust ROI/NPV based on risk.

Fields:

1. Confidence Level (High/Medium/Low)
2. Probability of Success (%)
3. Dependencies
4. Risk Adjustment Factor

Used to calculate:
Risk-Adjusted NPV = NPV × Probability of Success

------------------------------------------------------------
H. AI VALIDATION LAYER (AUTO)
------------------------------------------------------------
Agentic AI checks:

- Salary ranges vs HR data.
- Tool costs vs Finance data.
- Error rates vs Quality logs.
- Unrealistic savings or inflated assumptions.
- Missing cost categories.
- Missing cloud or subscription fees.
- Poor confidence levels.

AI Output:
- "Suggested Corrections"
- "Risks Identified"
- "Confidence Score"
- "Recommended Adjustments"

------------------------------------------------------------
3. BACKEND CALCULATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

ROI = (Total Benefits – Total Costs) ÷ Total Costs × 100

NPV Formula:
NPV = Σ (Cashflow_t ÷ (1 + Discount Rate)^t ) – Implementation Cost

Payback Period:
Months until cumulative savings exceed cost.

Cashflow:
Monthly_Savings – Monthly_Recurring_Cost

Sensitivity Analysis:
Run ±20% on savings and cost assumptions.
