
WalaPlus QMS – Agentic AI Duplicate Detection (Read-Only, Zoho-Aligned)

1. PURPOSE

This business case defines a new capability inside the WalaPlus QMS and Agentic AI framework to DETECT, ANALYZE, and REPORT duplicate Leads and Deals originating from Zoho CRM, without allowing the AI to take any write or update actions inside Zoho.


The Agentic AI will operate in READ-ONLY MODE and act as a governance, hygiene, and insight layer only.

All corrective actions (merge, close, reassignment) will remain human-driven and executed manually inside Zoho CRM based on AI recommendations.

------------------------------------------------------------

2. KEY CONSTRAINTS (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

- Zoho CRM is the system of record

- Agentic AI MUST NOT:
  • Merge records
  • Update records
  • Close records
  • Reassign ownership

- Agentic AI MUST:

  • Detect duplicates
  • Highlight risks
  • Recommend actions
  • Produce reports & exports
- Sandbox testing must be supported using FAKE / MOCK DATA
- Excel export must be available for operational follow-up

------------------------------------------------------------

3. BUSINESS PROBLEM

Duplicate Leads and Deals create:

- Inflated pipeline values
- Double-touching of the same customer
- Ownership conflicts (SDR vs Sales)
- Incorrect conversion rates
- Longer sales cycles
- Poor CRM hygiene
- Governance and audit gaps

Current Zoho duplicate rules are limited and do not:

- Work well across Arabic / English names
- Detect typos
- Correlate Lead ↔ Deal duplication
- Provide full journey and ownership timelines

------------------------------------------------------------

4. SOLUTION OVERVIEW
Introduce a new QMS module:

MODULE NAME:

“Duplicate Radar – Leads & Deals (Read-Only)”

This module uses Agentic AI to:

- Cluster Leads and Deals by COMPANY DOMAIN
- Detect probable duplicates using fuzzy logic
- Provide full lifecycle visibility
- Assign accountability
- Quantify business impact
- Support governance and quality KPIs

------------------------------------------------------------

5. DUPLICATE DETECTION LOGIC (AI RULES)

A. PRIMARY SIGNAL – COMPANY DOMAIN

- Extract domain from:
  • Lead email
  • Contact email
  • Deal-related email fields
  • Website field (if available)

- Normalize domain:

  • Lowercase
  • Remove “www.”
  • Remove trailing spaces
- Exclude public domains (gmail, yahoo, hotmail, outlook, etc.)

RULE:
All records sharing the same corporate domain belong to ONE company cluster, regardless of:
- Arabic vs English name
- Typo mistakes
- Different spellings
- Different lead sources

Example:
walaplus.com
→ “WalaPlus”
→ “ثروة العلم”
→ “Wala Pluss”

B. SECONDARY SIGNALS (SUPPORTING)

Used when domain is missing or ambiguous:
- Company name similarity (Arabic & English)
- Website similarity
- Phone number overlap
- City / country match

AI assigns a CONFIDENCE SCORE:
- High (≥90%)
- Medium (60–89%)
- Low (<60%)

------------------------------------------------------------

6. WHAT THE AGENTIC AI MUST HIGHLIGHT

A. DUPLICATE COUNTS

- Total duplicate Leads
- Total duplicate Deals
- Domains with both Leads & Deals duplicates
- Duplicates by source (Landing page, manual, import)
- Duplicates by owner / team

B. DOMAIN CLUSTER VIEW

For each company domain:

- Domain name
- All related Leads
- All related Deals
- Owners involved
- First created record (earliest date)
- Latest activity date
- Current status of each Lead
- Current stage of each Deal

C. FULL JOURNEY TIMELINE

Chronological timeline showing:

- Lead creation
- Deal creation
- Calls (from Five9 if available)
- Meetings (Google Calendar if available)
- Status changes

This timeline answers:
- Which record came first
- Which is active
- Which is redundant
- Who owns each record

------------------------------------------------------------

7. AI RECOMMENDATIONS (READ-ONLY)

For each duplicate cluster, the AI will recommend:

- Primary record (master)
- Secondary duplicate records
- Suggested action:
  • Merge (manual in Zoho)
  • Close as duplicate
  • Reassign owner
  • Correct company name
  • Correct domain/email

NO ACTION IS EXECUTED AUTOMATICALLY.

------------------------------------------------------------

8. OUTPUTS & EXPORTS

The system must allow:

- Excel export per domain cluster
- Excel export per owner
- Excel export per time period

- Columns include:

  • Record ID
  • Lead / Deal
  • Owner
  • Created date
  • Status / Stage
  • Domain
  • Duplicate confidence
  • AI recommendation

------------------------------------------------------------

9. SANDBOX & TESTING REQUIREMENTS

Inside QMS Sandbox:

- Ability to generate FAKE DATA:
  • 100+ Leads
  • 100+ Deals
  • Multiple domains
  • Intentional duplicates
  • Typo variations
  • Arabic / English names

- Ability to replay duplicate scenarios
- Ability to test AI confidence scoring
- Ability to test reporting & export

------------------------------------------------------------

10. GOVERNANCE & LOGGING

Every AI activity must be logged:

- Detection timestamp
- AI confidence score
- Recommendation given
- User who viewed/exported
- No silent processing

Logs must be:
- Immutable
- Searchable
- Audit-ready

------------------------------------------------------------

11. KPIs ENABLED BY THIS MODULE

- Duplicate Lead Rate (%)
- Duplicate Deal Rate (%)
- Domains with multiple active Deals
- Duplicate resolution SLA (manual follow-up)
- Duplicate rate by source
- Duplicate rate by owner
- Estimated pipeline inflation due to duplicates

14. FINAL IMPLEMENTATION INSTRUCTION

-----------------------------------------------------------

Semantic Search & Intelligent Navigation (Supportive Capability)

The system should support semantic search and similarity-based navigation to enhance user exploration and analysis of duplicate Leads and Deals.

Semantic capabilities are intended to:
- Improve navigation across duplicate clusters
- Support Arabic and English company name variations
- Handle typo and transliteration differences
- Enable natural-language exploration (e.g., “show similar companies”)

Semantic search SHALL NOT:
- Replace domain-based duplicate detection
- Automatically merge or modify records
- Override deterministic governance rules

Semantic indexing will be used as a supportive, read-only layer to assist users in discovering related records, clusters, and historical patterns.
