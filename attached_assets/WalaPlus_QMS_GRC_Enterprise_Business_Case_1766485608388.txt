PURPOSE
-------
This business case defines the missing enterprise-level Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) capabilities that must be added to the existing WalaPlus Quality Management System (QMS) after merging Quality & GRC under one Operations & Quality function.

The objective is to evolve the platform from a Quality Execution Tool into a full Enterprise Control Tower that supports:
• Executive governance
• Regulatory readiness
• Risk-based decision-making
• AI-driven assurance
• Audit-grade traceability
• SOP-driven operations

CURRENT STATE (WHAT EXISTS TODAY)
---------------------------------
The current QMS already covers:
• Internal quality audits & evaluations
• Deal / call / case quality scoring
• CAPA & nonconformance tracking
• Training assignment (basic)
• COPC Table F tracking
• ROI / NPV initiative calculator (basic)
• Quality dashboards & KPIs
• Agentic AI support for call & quality analysis

GAP STATEMENT
-------------
The system does NOT yet fully cover:
• Enterprise risk management
• Policy lifecycle governance
• Regulatory compliance tracking
• Vendor & third-party risk
• External audit readiness
• Control framework mapping (ISO / SOA)
• Executive risk reporting
• Full AI governance orchestration
• End-to-end audit-grade logs
• GRC role ownership workflows

TARGET STATE (WHAT MUST BE BUILT)
---------------------------------

1) ENTERPRISE RISK MANAGEMENT MODULE
-----------------------------------
Build a centralized Enterprise Risk Register that covers:
• Operational risks
• Legal risks
• Financial risks
• Data privacy (PDPL)
• Information security
• Fraud
• Vendor / third-party risks

Risk Lifecycle:
• Risk identification
• Risk categorization
• Impact scoring (1–5)
• Likelihood scoring (1–5)
• Auto risk score calculation
• Risk owner assignment (BU / IT / Ops / Vendor)
• Risk treatment (Mitigate / Accept / Transfer / Avoid)
• Residual risk tracking
• Review cadence & escalation

AI Role:
• Detect risks from audits, incidents, calls, and compliance gaps
• Auto-suggest treatments
• Flag overdue or escalating risks
• Predict high-risk exposure trends

2) POLICY & DOCUMENT GOVERNANCE MODULE
-------------------------------------
Build a policy governance engine with:
• Central policy library (single source of truth)
• Policy lifecycle:
  Draft → Review → Approval → Publish → Enforce → Annual Review
• Version control
• Change history
• Policy ownership
• Policy-to-risk mapping
• Policy acknowledgment tracking per BU

AI Role:
• Detect outdated policies
• Highlight missing policies vs regulations
• Assist in drafting updates

3) COMPLIANCE & REGULATORY TRACKER
---------------------------------
Track compliance with:
• PDPL
• NCA / ECC
• ISO 27001
• PCI (readiness roadmap)

Features:
• Regulatory obligation register
• Compliance checklist per regulation
• Evidence attachment per obligation
• Compliance status (Compliant / Partial / Gap)
• Regulatory calendar & reminders

AI Role:
• Identify missing evidence
• Predict audit readiness gaps
• Generate compliance summaries

4) VENDOR & THIRD-PARTY RISK MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------
Features:
• Vendor inventory
• Vendor risk assessments
• Critical vendor classification
• Risk acceptance or mitigation workflows
• Vendor audit tracking
• Evidence & remediation tracking

AI Role:
• Score vendor risk
• Flag high-risk vendors
• Track remediation deadlines

5) AUDIT READINESS & FINDINGS GOVERNANCE
---------------------------------------
Support:
• External audits
• Regulatory audits
• Client audits

Features:
• Audit planning calendar
• Audit scope definition
• Findings register
• Owner assignment
• Deadline tracking
• Closure validation

AI Role:
• Track evidence completeness
• Predict audit failure risk
• Generate audit packs

6) QUALITY ↔ GRC ↔ AI HANDOFF ENGINE
-----------------------------------
Mandatory workflow:
Internal Quality Finding
→ Risk Statement (GRC)
→ Control Mapping
→ Action Plan
→ Evidence Collection
→ Closure Validation
→ Executive Reporting

No finding, risk, or action is allowed to exist outside the system.

7) EXECUTIVE GOVERNANCE & RISK REPORTING
---------------------------------------
Dashboards for:
• MBR
• QBR
• ABR
• CEO / Board reviews

Include:
• Top enterprise risks
• Risk trends
• Compliance posture
• Audit readiness score
• Governance maturity index

ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES
------------------------

GRC MANAGER ROLE
• Own enterprise governance framework
• Own risk register & compliance trackers
• Own policy lifecycle
• Act as regulator & auditor focal point
• Own external audit readiness
• Review outputs, not manual execution

QUALITY MANAGER ROLE
• Own internal audits
• Own quality CAPA
• Feed risks into GRC module
• Execute process improvements

AI SPECIALIST (AGENTIC AI)
• Monitor all workflows
• Track deadlines & owners
• Collect & validate evidence
• Detect gaps & risks
• Generate insights & recommendations
• Maintain immutable logs
• Reduce manual follow-up

DESIGN & UX DIRECTION
---------------------
Design principles:
• Executive-first
• Simple language
• Progressive disclosure (Show More)
• No clutter
• Fool-proof navigation

Top Navigation:
Dashboard | Quality | GRC | Risks | Compliance | Audits | ROI | AI Insights | Admin

Key UX Components:
• Control Tower Home
• Risk Heatmaps
• Policy Library
• Compliance Checklists
• Audit Readiness View
• AI Insights Panel
• Full Activity Logs

LOGS & TRACEABILITY (MANDATORY)
-------------------------------
System must log:
• Every create / update / delete
• Before & after values
• User identity
• Timestamp
• Source (Human / AI)

Logs must be:
• Immutable
• Searchable
• Exportable
• Audit-grade

SOP UPDATE REQUIREMENT
----------------------
Replit must:
• Update all SOPs to reflect new modules
• Ensure SOPs map to system workflows
• Ensure AI actions are documented
• Ensure governance ownership is clear
• Ensure no process exists outside the system

SUCCESS CRITERIA
----------------
• Single source of truth for Quality & GRC
• No Excel-based risk or compliance tracking
• Audit readiness at any time
• Clear ownership & accountability
• AI-driven governance, not manual chasing
• Executive confidence in reports
