What does this mean?

  • Junk records (score 0-20) are quarantined from duplicate detection -- they have gibberish names, fake data, or are clearly spam entries.
  • Poor records (score 21-49) are included in detection but may produce false positives due to missing fields.
  • Fair records (score 50-79) have some missing fields but enough data for reliable matching.
  • Good records (score 80-100) have complete, clean data for high-confidence duplicate detection.
