The Future of Automated Data Hygiene, Governance, and Operational Excellence at WalaPlus**

As WalaPlus continues its digital transformation journey, one of the most critical challenges across all commercial teams — Sales, SDR, Customer Success, Marketplace, and Operations — is the ongoing issue of data hygiene, CRM discipline, and governance adherence. Despite the existence of SOPs, Governance Documents, and clear processes, inconsistencies still occur due to human error, time pressure, or lack of visibility.

To ensure accuracy, consistency, and operational excellence, WalaPlus is introducing a groundbreaking concept:

🚀 The Agentic AI Quality Specialist

A fully autonomous AI-powered specialist designed to operate across Zoho CRM, Google Calendar, Five9, and GCP Cloud, performing the full scope of quality auditing, data hygiene checking, and governance validation — exactly like a human Quality Specialist, but faster, unbiased, and available 24/7.

This AI becomes the digital backbone for People, Process, and Governance scoring across the entire commercial ecosystem.

🎯 Purpose of the Agentic AI Quality Specialist

The goal is to automate the entire quality lifecycle from A to Z, ensuring that data is clean, processes are followed, governance rules are respected, and performance is accurately measured.

The AI will:

Continuously monitor CRM and system activity

Detect hygiene issues

Correct violations

Validate compliance

Score individuals and teams

Enforce governance

Provide insights and RCA

Link activities across systems

Produce weekly quality dashboards

In short, it will serve as a 24/7 digital auditor and governance enforcer.

🧠 How the AI Operates (End-to-End Automation)
1. Continuous Data Hygiene Monitoring Across the Ecosystem

The AI checks every action made by humans across:

Zoho CRM (leads, deals, notes, tasks, calls, emails)

Google Calendar (meetings, attendance, follow-up commitments)

Five9 (call logs, dispositions, attempts, missed calls)

GCP Cloud (data storage, logs, AI processing)

It detects issues such as:

Missing mandatory fields

Incorrect pipeline stages

Leads without activities

Duplicated records

Missing call notes

Meetings not logged in CRM

Incorrect SDR qualification fields

Missing proposals or attachments

No follow-ups scheduled

Governance non-compliance

Incomplete ticket tagging

SLA breaches

Wrong deal ownership

This replaces manual sampling with 100% full coverage auditing.

2. Automatic Correction & Standardization (Self-Cleaning CRM)

Based on our uploaded Governance Documents, the AI automatically:

Updates wrong fields

Merges duplicates

Standardizes industry segments

Fixes lead sources

Formats email/phone correctly

Moves deals to the correct stage

Adds missing follow-up tasks

Assigns records to the correct owner

Tags opportunities using correct rules

Uploads missing reminders

Ensures call → CRM linkage

Ensures meeting → CRM linkage

Zoho CRM becomes self-cleaning and self-correcting.

3. Cross-System Validation (CRM ↔ Calendar ↔ Five9)

AI ensures that all activities match across systems:

If a meeting exists in Google Calendar → must be logged in CRM.

If a call exists in Five9 → notes must be in CRM.

If a follow-up is scheduled → CRM must reflect it.

If an SDR qualifies a lead → required governance fields must be filled.

If Sales moves a deal stage → required documents must be attached.

This ends the problem of activities happening outside the CRM.

4. Real-Time Alerts & Escalations

AI notifies the right person at the right time, such as:

“Meeting completed 2 hours ago — CRM not updated.”
“Lead in Qualification for 4 days with no activity.”
“Duplicate record detected — fixed automatically.”
“Call logged with no disposition — corrected.”

Alerts go to SDR, Sales, CS, Marketplace, Team Leads, or Managers depending on the issue.

5. Weekly Quality Scoring Engine (People — Process — Governance)

The AI generates a transparent, unbiased, automated score for each dimension:

A) People Score

Evaluates each employee based on:

Data hygiene discipline

Follow-up accuracy

Correctness of CRM entries

Notes completeness & quality

Linking calls to CRM

Linking meetings to CRM

Adherence to activity timelines

Number of AI corrections required per agent

This becomes a monthly KPI for:

SDR

Sales

Customer Success

Marketplace

Team Leads

B) Process Score

Evaluates how well teams follow:

SOPs

Workflow steps

Deal movement rules

Qualification criteria

Follow-up timelines

Escalation matrix

SLA timelines

Ticket lifecycle movement

“No shortcut” rules inside workflows

This gives visibility to managers and HODs on process maturity.

C) Governance Score

Because WalaPlus already has Governance Documents, we will upload them to train the AI.
The AI will check:

Naming conventions

Mandatory fields

Tagging rules

Required attachments

Compliance with SOP terms

Correct qualification criteria

Correct documentation for every stage

Data consistency across teams

Governance becomes measurable and enforceable.
