12. ROI & NPV EVALUATION ENGINE (NEW)
---------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE:
To allow Quality Department to evaluate the financial attractiveness of initiatives, projects, or improvements across WalaPlus.

INPUT FIELDS:
1. Project Name
2. Owner
3. Department
4. Baseline Cost (SAR)
5. Expected Savings (SAR/month)
6. One-time Implementation Cost
7. Project Duration (months)
8. Discount Rate (company default or manual)
9. Expected Revenue Increase (optional)
10. Avoided Cost (optional)

OUTPUTS (AUTO-CALCULATED):
→ ROI %
ROI = (Total Gains – Total Cost) / Total Cost

→ Payback Period
Payback Period = Initial Cost / Monthly Savings

→ NPV (Net Present Value)
NPV = Σ (Cash Flow_t / (1+r)^t ) – Initial Investment

→ Project Profitability Index
PI = NPV / Initial Cost

→ AI Insights:
• “This initiative exceeds minimum ROI threshold.”
• “Payback is under 6 months — strong candidate.”
• “NPV negative — not recommended.”
• “Long-term strategic benefit outweighs short-term cost.”
• “High risk due to benefit uncertainty.”

DASHBOARD VIEW:
• List of all initiatives
• ROI ranking
• NPV ranking
• Filter by department or owner
• AI recommendation badge (Approve / Evaluate / Reject)
