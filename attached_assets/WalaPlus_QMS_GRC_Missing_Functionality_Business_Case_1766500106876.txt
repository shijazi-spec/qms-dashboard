WALAPLUS – QMS & GRC KPI ENGINE EXTENSION
BUSINESS CASE FOR REPLIT (DOWNLOADABLE NOTEPAD)

1. PURPOSE
This document defines the missing functional, governance, AI, and KPI capabilities required to fully support the merged Quality & GRC operating model at WalaPlus. It translates the 2026 Quality & GRC KPI framework into system-buildable requirements for Replit.

2. CURRENT STATE (WHAT EXISTS)
The current QMS system includes:
- Basic project creation and assignment
- Training placeholders
- ROI initiative tracking
- Table F governance logic
- High-level dashboards
- Manual KPI views

However, the system does NOT include an enterprise KPI execution engine, risk lifecycle management, compliance tracking, or AI-enabled governance logic.

3. IDENTIFIED GAPS (WHAT IS NOT COVERED)
The following critical capabilities are missing:

A. KPI ENGINE
- Central KPI catalog with ownership
- Automated KPI calculations
- Threshold logic (Green/Amber/Red)
- Historical KPI trends
- Evidence linkage
- KPI definitions and formulas
- KPI frequency and review cycles

B. ROLE-BASED OWNERSHIP
- Separation of KPIs by role:
  * Quality Manager (Sara)
  * GRC Manager (Maram)
  * Shared KPIs
- No accountability mapping today

C. RISK MANAGEMENT
- No enterprise risk register
- No risk lifecycle (identify, assess, treat, close)
- No risk scoring or ownership
- No linkage between risks, audits, controls, and CAPAs

D. COMPLIANCE & REGULATORY
- No PDPL / NCA / ECC / ISO 27001 compliance matrix
- No control library
- No evidence repository
- No compliance coverage scoring

E. AUDIT GOVERNANCE
- No audit findings lifecycle
- No SLA-based closure tracking
- No repeat finding detection
- No audit-readiness scoring

F. VENDOR & THIRD-PARTY RISK
- No vendor inventory
- No vendor risk assessments
- No remediation tracking
- No evidence storage

G. AI ENABLEMENT
- No AI operational role
- No AI validation or drift monitoring
- No AI-based insights for risks, audits, or KPIs

H. EXECUTIVE VISIBILITY
- No CEO/Board-ready dashboards
- No MBR/QBR/ABR summaries
- No trend and exposure analysis

4. REQUIRED SYSTEM MODULES TO BUILD

A. KPI ENGINE MODULE
- KPI Catalog:
  * Name
  * Owner (Quality / GRC / Shared)
  * Description
  * Formula
  * Data source
  * Frequency
  * Thresholds
- Automated calculation engine
- Manual override with justification
- Evidence attachment
- Trend tracking
- KPI-to-risk and KPI-to-audit linkage

- Governance coverage

- Repeat findings reduction

- Training coverage
- Automation coverage

GRC Manager KPIs:

- Compliance coverage
- Audit readiness

Shared KPIs:

- Risk-to-control mapping

5. UI / UX DIRECTION
Principles:
- Executive-first design
- Clean, simple, and intuitive
- Progressive disclosure (Show More)
- No clutter, no Excel-like views

Navigation:
Dashboard | QMS | Table F | Risk | Compliance | Audits | ROI | Team | AI Insights | Admin

KPI Views:
- Summary cards (Green/Amber/Red)

  * Definition

  * AI insights

Risk Views:

AI Banner Across System:
“AI reviewed this data – Confidence: XX%”

6. SOP & GOVERNANCE REQUIREMENTS
- Every action must be logged:
  * Create
  * Update
  * Delete
  * Approve
- Logs must capture:
  * User
  * Timestamp
  * Before/after state
- SOPs must be:
  * Linked to workflows
  * Auto-updated when processes change
- No action without audit trail

7. IMPLEMENTATION INSTRUCTION
- Build all features inside the existing QMS (no separate system)
- Extend Team, KPI, Audit, Risk, Compliance, and AI modules
- Ensure role-based access control
- Ensure audit-readiness at all times

8. FINAL STATEMENT
This extension transforms QMS into an Enterprise Governance, Risk, and Assurance Platform with AI at the core, supporting executive decision-making, regulatory readiness, and scalable growth.
