Domain clusters. Groups of records that share the same company domain (e.g. acme.com) across one or more Zoho modules. Each cluster is a candidate merge: open one to see the underlying Leads / Deals / Contacts / Accounts, decide which record should survive, and take action in Zoho. Filter by module or cluster type to focus on a specific cleanup batch.
Lead duplicates. Leads in Zoho that share an email, phone, or company domain with another lead — the highest-volume source of duplicate creation. Resolving these prevents two reps from working the same prospect and stops the same company from appearing twice in pipeline reports.
Lead Dup signal Email Phone Layout Owner Status Created
Deal duplicates. Deals on the same Account (or same company domain) that look like they describe the same opportunity. Critical to clean up before quarter-end because each duplicate deal inflates pipeline value and skews forecast accuracy.
Deal Dup signal Stage Value Layout Owner Created
Contact duplicates. Contact records that share an email address, phone, or full name within the same Account. Merging these keeps activity history (calls, emails, meetings) attached to a single person instead of scattered across copies, and prevents duplicate outreach.
Contact Dup signal Email Phone Layout Owner Created
Account duplicates. Account records that appear to describe the same company — same domain, same legal name, or close fuzzy-name match. Resolving these is the foundation of clean reporting: once Accounts are unique, related Contacts and Deals roll up correctly under one customer.
Account Dup signal Email Phone Website Layout Owner Created
