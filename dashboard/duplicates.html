Duplicate Creation Trend

New duplicate records created PER WEEK — a leading indicator of how well your prevention work is keeping bad data out of Zoho. Falling line = prevention is working. Compare against the 2% target on the right-hand cards.

How to read this chart
  • Red filled area — "New duplicates": how many NEW records added to Zoho this week landed in a duplicate cluster. Lower = better.
  • Grey dashed line — "New records (total)": how many new records came in total this week. Context for the red line.
  • Blue line — "Duplicate rate %" (right axis): red ÷ grey × 100. The headline metric. Target ≤ 2%.
  • Latest bucket = the most recent week. Window total = sum across the whole period selected above (4 / 12 / 26 / 52 weeks).
  • Reading the trend: a falling red area + falling blue line = prevention is winning. A rising line means new bad data is still flowing in faster than you're cleaning the existing pile.

Cross-module overlaps — actionable work only

Clusters where the same company appears across multiple Zoho modules AND there is something to do here. We surface only the cases that have a Zoho action available on this tab:

  • Lead ↔ Active Deal — close the redundant Lead, Sales is already pursuing it.

  • Contact ↔ Deal — LINK by setting Contact_Name on the Deal.

Pure Lead ↔ Contact and Lead ↔ Account overlaps are not shown — Zoho has no field to link a Lead to anything, so the only action is CLOSE the Lead, which is handled directly in the Leads Duplicates tab. Pre-import check. Paste your incoming lead/deal list (CSV with a domain column, or a JSON array) and click Check. Each row is matched against existing CRM data and gets a verdict so you don't push duplicates or active customers as fresh leads.
📋 How a row is screened — rejection rules (click to expand)

Scope: corporate / B2B only. Marketplace & merchant records (layouts Marketplace / Partner Accounts) are out of scope and pass through.

Two basic rules run, in order. A row is rejected on the first one it hits; if neither hits, it PASSES.

Rule 1 — is this contact a duplicate? (checked first)

  • REJECT (duplicate) — the row's email or phone number already exists on any CRM record. The contact is already on file → do not import.

Rule 2 — is the company already a customer? (only if Rule 1 finds nothing)

  • REJECT (existing customer) — the company domain has a Deal in Agreement Signed or Paid (strictly these two stages) with no churn date. Already our customer → do not import; route to CS.

No duplicate email/phone and no signed/paid-no-churn deal on the domain → PASS (safe to import).

The earlier expanded ruleset (active leads, open deals, closed-lost link, churn cool-off, company-name fuzzy, signal-strength downgrades) is archived for now and can be switched back on later.
