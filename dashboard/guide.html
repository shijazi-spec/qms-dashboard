3. AI Quality Specialist

What is the AI Quality Specialist?

The AI Quality Specialist is an autonomous AI agent powered by GPT-4o that automatically audits your business data every week. It runs on its own - you don't need to do anything!

What Does the AI Audit?

Leads

Checks for missing emails, phone numbers, owner assignment, lead source

Deals

Validates deal amount, stage, expected close date, contact association

Contacts

Verifies email format, phone number, mailing address, account link

Tasks

Checks for overdue tasks, missing due dates, priority assignment

How the AI Audit Works

1

Every Monday at 8 AM (UTC)

The system automatically wakes up and starts the audit process. You don't need to do anything.

2

Connects to Zoho CRM

The AI securely connects to your Zoho CRM and downloads all your Leads, Deals, Contacts, and Tasks data.

3

Checks Google Calendar

It also looks at your calendar events from the past 7 days to check meeting compliance.

4

AI Analysis with GPT-4o

The AI Quality Specialist analyzes all the data, looking for problems like missing information, duplicate records, stale deals, and process gaps.

5

Calculates Quality Score

Based on what it finds, the AI calculates an overall quality score from 0-100, broken down into three dimensions:

People
25% weight
Process
35% weight
Governance
40% weight
6

Generates Recommendations

The AI creates specific, actionable recommendations to fix the issues it found.

7

Sends Email Report

A beautiful email report is automatically sent to amashah@outlook.com with all the findings and recommendations.

8

Updates Dashboard

All results are saved and appear on the Quality Dashboard at / for you to review anytime.

15. Quality-GRC Handoffs

What are Handoffs?

Handoffs are automatic connections between Quality Management and GRC. When something happens in Quality, the system automatically creates a corresponding record in GRC - no manual work needed!

Automatic Handoff Rules

CAPA Record
Quality Module
When Severity = High or Critical
Risk Created
GRC Risk Register
Example: Sara creates a CAPA for "Customer data exposed in test environment" with severity "Critical". The system automatically creates a Risk in the Risk Register so Maram can track and treat it.
Low Sentiment Call
Call Intelligence
When Sentiment Score < 3
Compliance Gap
Compliance Tracker
Example: A customer call is analyzed by AI and gets a sentiment score of 2 (poor). The system flags this as a potential compliance issue for customer service standards.
Nonconformance
QMS Module
Any nonconformance recorded
Audit Finding
Audit Readiness
Example: Sara records a nonconformance "Process not followed for customer onboarding". This automatically appears as an Audit Finding so it's documented for the next audit.
Overdue Training
Training Compliance
When training is overdue
Compliance Gap
Compliance Tracker
Example: Employee Ahmed's "Data Privacy Training" is 30 days overdue. This creates a compliance gap against PDPL requirements for staff training.

Dual Ownership for Policies

Operational Owner

Responsible for day-to-day implementation and content accuracy. Usually a department head or process owner.

  • • Drafts policy content
  • • Implements procedures
  • • Tracks operational compliance

Compliance Owner (GRC)

Ensures policy meets regulatory requirements and governance standards. Always a GRC team member.

  • • Reviews for regulatory compliance
  • • Approves for publication
  • • Manages audit evidence
Policy Publishing Workflow

A policy cannot be published until both the Operational Owner has drafted/reviewed it AND the Compliance Owner (GRC) has approved it. This ensures both operational accuracy and regulatory compliance.

Track real-time quality performance of your team members based on their CRM Lead Owner data. This feature shows how well each agent maintains data quality for the records they own.

Where to Find It: Quality Dashboard (/) → Agent Performance Widget

Understanding Agent Scores

How Scores Are Calculated

Each agent's score is based on the data quality of CRM records they own:

  • Critical Issues: Missing email, invalid data (4x weight)
  • High Issues: Missing lead source (3x weight)
  • Medium Issues: Incomplete fields (2x weight)
  • Low Issues: Minor formatting (1x weight)

18. Multi-Scorecard Governance NEW in v2.1

Create and manage multiple named scorecards per team with versioning. Each team (SDR, Sales, CS, Marketplace) can have different evaluation criteria tailored to their specific needs.

Where to Find It: Admin Panel (/admin) → Scorecards Tab

Scorecard Components

Scorecard Properties

  • ●
    Name: Descriptive name (e.g., "SDR Lead Quality v1.0")
  • ●
    Module/Team: SDR, Sales, CS, or Marketplace
  • ●
    Version: Track changes (v1.0, v1.1, v2.0)
  • ●
    Active Status: Only one scorecard per team can be active

Attribute Fields

  • ●
    Dimension: People, Process, or Governance
  • ●
    Severity: Critical, Major, or Minor
  • ●
    Evaluation Logic: How the AI should evaluate
  • ●
    Evidence Source: Which CRM fields to check

How to Manage Scorecards

1
Select Module/Team
Use the dropdown to choose which team's scorecards to view
2
Create or Select Scorecard
Click "Create New" or select an existing scorecard
3
Add/Edit Attributes
Define evaluation criteria with severity, logic, and evidence sources
4
Set Active
Toggle "Active" to make this the default scorecard for AI audits
5
Clone for New Version
Use "Clone" to create a new version when making significant changes
AI Integration: When audits run, the AI agent automatically loads the active scorecard for each team, uses the evaluation logic, and generates findings based on your defined criteria.

19. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need to run the AI audit manually?

No! The AI Quality Specialist runs automatically every Monday at 8 AM UTC. You just need to check the results on the dashboard or in your email.

Q: Will the AI change my CRM data?

No. The AI only reads your data - it never modifies, deletes, or changes anything in Zoho CRM. Your data is completely safe.

Q: What if I don't have Zoho CRM credentials configured?

The system will gracefully handle this. You'll see a message on the dashboard, and you can still use all other features like CAPA, Training, and GRC modules.

Q: How do handoffs work?

When you create certain records in Quality (like a Critical CAPA), the system automatically creates corresponding records in GRC (like a Risk). You don't need to do anything extra.

Q: Can I import data from Excel or CSV?

Yes! Use the Data Migration module at /migration. Upload your file, and the AI will help map your columns to the correct fields.

Q: What regulations does the Compliance Tracker support?

We have 6 pre-configured frameworks: PDPL (Saudi data protection), NCA-ECC (Saudi cybersecurity), NCA-DCC (Saudi data controls), ISO 9001 (Quality), ISO 27001 (Security), and COPC (Customer operations).

Q: What do the colors mean on the dashboards?

Green = Good/Compliant
Yellow = Warning/Needs Attention
Red = Critical/Urgent

Q: How do I print this guide?

Click the "Print Guide" button at the top of this page, or press Ctrl+P (Windows) or Cmd+P (Mac). You can save it as a PDF for offline reference.

Q: Why can't I accept a risk or approve a policy?

Risk acceptance and policy approval are GRC-only functions that require the GRC Manager role (Maram). If you need a risk accepted or policy approved, contact your GRC Manager. This is by design to ensure proper governance oversight - "AI detects, humans act with proper authorization."

Q: What are the different user roles in the system?

There are 6 roles: Quality Manager (Sara), GRC Manager (Maram), AI Specialist, Business Unit Owner, Executive, and Admin. Each role has specific permissions. GRC Manager has authority over risk acceptance and policy approval. See Section 16 for the full permission matrix.
