1. Executive Summary

Purpose

The ExampleOrg Enterprise GRC & Quality Management Platform is a comprehensive, AI-powered system combining governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) with quality management. It provides autonomous monitoring, governance validation, quality scoring, enterprise risk management, policy governance, compliance tracking, and vendor risk assessment across ExampleOrg's commercial ecosystem.

Key Objectives

  • Automate quality audits across CRM data (Leads, Deals, Contacts, Tasks)
  • Validate calendar events and meeting compliance
  • Generate AI-powered insights and recommendations
  • Provide real-time dashboard visualization
  • Enable administrative governance management
  • Support ISO 9001, COPC, and Six Sigma quality frameworks
  • Enterprise Risk Register with risk scoring matrix and treatment workflows
  • Policy & Document Governance with lifecycle management and version control
  • Compliance Tracker for PDPL, NCA-ECC, and ISO regulatory frameworks
  • Audit Readiness with findings register and evidence pack generation
  • Vendor Risk Management with assessments and remediation tracking
  • Data Migration Engine with AI-powered field mapping and deduplication
  • GRC Control Tower executive dashboard with cross-module integration

Target Users

User Type Access Level Primary Functions
Quality Managers Full Access View dashboards, analyze trends, generate reports
Administrators Admin Panel Manage governance rules, scorecards, system settings
Executives Dashboard View high-level quality metrics and KPIs

10.8 GRC Control Tower

Dashboard: /grc

The GRC Control Tower is the executive command center providing a unified view across all GRC modules with real-time metrics, cross-module integration, and strategic decision support.

Dashboard Sections

  • Risk Heat Map: Visual 4x4 grid showing risk distribution by likelihood and impact
  • Compliance Overview: Scores by framework (PDPL, NCA-ECC, ISO, COPC)
  • Policy Lifecycle: Status breakdown (Draft, Review, Approval, Published, Retired)
  • Audit Status: Upcoming audits, open findings by severity
  • Vendor Risk: Critical vendors, expiring contracts, pending assessments
  • Handoff Automation: Active rules, recent events, control effectiveness
