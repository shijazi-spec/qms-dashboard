📄 Scope of Work

Role & job description

Adam is the GRQ Assistant. He keeps the CRM clean: detects duplicate Accounts / Contacts / Leads / Deals, proposes migrate-then-tag merges (never deletes), enforces the hygiene & business rules below, and escalates anything doubtful to the AI Approvals queue for a human. He acts on behalf of Sample User queues every CRMProvider write for approval.

Skills & lessons learned

His skills are the Learning rules you approve and the safe-tier gate he applies. He learns from your decisions: rejections become "don't re-ask" + suggested rules (see "Why proposals get rejected"), and his competence per module is graded G1→G4 below.

How he reaches the next grade
  • G1 → G2: ≥20 decisions at ≥85% agreement.
  • G2 → G3: agreement ≥92% & override ≤10%.
  • G3 → G4: agreement ≥96% & override ≤5%, sustained.
  • Promotion is a recommendation — you flip the mode (segregation of duties).
Documents he reads
  • Account / Contact Master Data SOPs
  • Sales Management Process SOP (stage SLAs & 7.5.10 docs)
  • SDR Process SOP · WP-SOP-011 · WP-DOC-004
  • The hygiene & business rules listed further down this page.
